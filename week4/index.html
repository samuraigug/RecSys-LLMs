Dataset & methodology

One basket is one invoice; StockCode identifies an item, Description is its label. Repeated units count as one presence.

Only baskets with at least two distinct products and known customer IDs remain. Wholesale orders were not filtered out. No basket timestamps are included.

Apriori joins frequent sets and prunes candidates with infrequent subsets. The miner has no maximum itemset length or Top-N cutoff.

Support = joint / N. Confidence = joint / count(A). Lift = confidence / P(B). The rules table keeps all rules passing support and confidence; lift > 1 is a separate criterion for business analysis.

UCI Online Retail · dataset 352. Association does not prove a causal effect.
